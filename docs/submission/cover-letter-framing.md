Dear [Editor / Program Chair],

I am submitting "Digital Proprioception and Allostatic Load: A Deployed Synthetic-System Report on the Cumulative-Deviation Hypothesis" for consideration as a deployed synthetic-systems report with empirical case studies and hypothesis-generating biological analogies.

The manuscript should not be read as a clinical validation paper, a computational-psychiatry paper, or a claim that biological allostatic load has been reproduced in software. Its central claim is narrower: UNITARES provides an intervention-coupled, continuously observable control-signal analogue of the cumulative-deviation form that allostatic-load theory has long made mathematically central but that clinical measurement usually observes only sparsely and indirectly.

The paper makes four bounded contributions:

1. It formalizes the Anima Void Integral, $V_{\text{anima}}$, as a deployed cumulative-deviation control-signal analogue inspired by allostatic load, while explicitly excluding endocrine, immune, tissue-damage, and clinical-allostatic-load claims.
2. It reports a class-conditional calibration result in production telemetry, including a same-row formula-vs-calibration ablation that bounds the 28.9% basin-flip result rather than treating it as a clean causal decomposition.
3. It imports McEwen's Four Types as a useful but non-exhaustive failure-mode taxonomy for AI-agent governance.
4. It develops a synthetic-psychology stance in which deployed agents generate and stress-test informational hypotheses about adaptive systems without claiming biological identity.

The most important case study is deliberately framed as bounded evidence. An 86-minute Lumen observation window initially appeared Type-3-like under a stale Phase 2 calibration anchor. The disambiguating recalibration instead resolves the case as a candidate substrate-associated basin transition. The manuscript treats this as provenance-backed single-agent case-report evidence for the transition's date, magnitude, and shape, and as anomaly-grade evidence for substrate causality pending artifact release, independent audit, or replication.

The clinical-facing sections are study sketches and hypothesis generators, not turnkey protocols. They identify where longitudinal clinical measurement might test analogous baseline-anchoring or calibration-staleness questions, while leaving cohort design, clinical endpoints, statistical power, privacy, consent, and regulatory questions to future work.

Because some production telemetry cannot yet be released in raw form, the manuscript separates pipeline reproducibility from production-number verification and independent validation. Self-citations are used for system provenance and artifact lineage, not as substitutes for independent biological or clinical validation.

For these reasons, I believe the manuscript is best reviewed by readers comfortable with AI governance, artificial life, complex adaptive systems, runtime monitoring, and cautious biological analogy. It is not positioned as evidence that the biological mechanisms of allostatic load have been instantiated or clinically validated in an AI system.
